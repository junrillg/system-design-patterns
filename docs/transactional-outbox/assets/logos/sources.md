## Guidance encountered

- **Django:** Django and its logo are registered trademarks of the Django Software Foundation. [Logo standards](https://www.djangoproject.com/community/logos/) require original colors, proportions and separation from added text. [Trademark agreement, section 1](https://www.djangoproject.com/trademarks/) discusses identification of Django-related services/tools and linking the logo to Django’s site wherever technically possible.
- **Python:** the logo belongs to the Python Software Foundation. The [logo page](https://www.python.org/community/logos/) permits the standalone two-snakes mark on the same terms as the combined logo; the [trademark policy](https://www.python.org/psf/trademarks/) discusses unaltered logos and nominative use. The logo was designed by Tim Parkin.
- **PostgreSQL:** PostgreSQL and Slonik are trademarks or registered trademarks of the PostgreSQL Community Association of Canada. The [trademark policy](https://www.postgresql.org/about/policies/trademarks/) governs use, requires unchanged marks and cautions against presenting them with other marks. This is not a claim of explicit permission from that association.
- **RabbitMQ (not included):** RabbitMQ is a trademark of Broadcom, Inc. in the U.S. and other countries. Its [guidelines](https://www.rabbitmq.com/trademark-guidelines) distinguish nominative name use from logo use and state that logo use is reserved solely for Broadcom. The download’s availability is not a blanket license. The diagram therefore uses a generic queue symbol and a written RabbitMQ label. No permission or endorsement is claimed here.
- **Celery:** the [official documentation](https://docs.celeryq.dev/en/stable/) identifies the project as BSD-licensed; this alone does not establish a separate trademark license for the logo. No logo-specific permission claim is made.

The SVG files were checked for scripts, foreign objects and external resource references; none were present. Original files are retained without redrawing or recoloring. Their enclosing educational diagram may scale them proportionally.
